How It Works

From a blank page to a controlled document

01

Tell us what you do

Answer a handful of plain-language questions about the process, who it's for, and where it runs.

02

We structure the procedure

Purpose, scope, applicability, requirements, and responsibilities get built from your answers, not a generic template.

03

We build the visuals

Process diagrams and supporting visuals get generated directly from your procedure's own steps and roles.

04

We assemble the document

Table of contents, KPIs, records, references, and annexures, all in a formatted, controlled-document layout.

05

You review and download

Read it in the browser, edit anything that's off, then export a real DOCX or PDF ready to circulate.
