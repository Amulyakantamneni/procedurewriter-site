About

Procedures fail when they're written once and never read

Most SOPs are written to satisfy an audit, filed away, and ignored until the next audit. We think the point of a procedure is that someone follows it — so we build documents for the person doing the work, not just the reviewer checking the box.

A procedure nobody follows isn't a procedure — it's a liability with a version number.

Our Approach

AI drafts. People decide what's true.

We use AI to move fast from raw material to a structured first draft — but nothing goes to a client without a person checking it against the actual regulation and the actual process.

Human-reviewed, always

Every document is checked by a person before delivery — not just spell-checked, but verified against the source process and the relevant compliance framework.

Confidential by design

Process details, especially in regulated industries, are sensitive. We scope exactly what's needed for each engagement and sign an NDA before anything is shared.

Written for the reader

A procedure a new hire can't follow on their first try isn't finished. We write for the person doing the task, not just for the file cabinet.

One template, every team

Consistent structure across your organization, so a procedure from one department reads the same way as one from another — easier to audit, easier to maintain.

Written with the people who do the work

Every draft is checked against how the process actually runs on the ground — not just how it looks on paper.
