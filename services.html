Services

Documentation services for teams that get audited

Every engagement starts with your existing process — interviews, raw notes, old documents — and ends with a version-controlled procedure your team will actually use.

Six services, one consistent standard

Whichever service you start with, every document follows the same template — so it's easy to audit and easy to maintain later.

SOP Creation & Standardization

We draft complete Standard Operating Procedures from interviews, raw notes, or existing documents — structured, consistent, and ready to deploy. Every draft follows a single template across your organization, so a procedure written for one team reads the same way as one written for another.

Compliance Documentation

Procedures mapped directly to the frameworks your auditors check — HIPAA, ISO 9001, OSHA, FDA, SOC 2 — with the specific clause or control referenced inline, so reviewers can trace every step back to a requirement.

Process Mapping & Workflow Documentation

Visual, step-by-step workflow diagrams for processes that cross multiple teams or systems — the kind of process that currently only makes sense to the one person who's been doing it for years.

AI Governance & Model-Use Procedures

Operating procedures for teams deploying AI internally — prompt and data-handling guidelines, human-review checkpoints, and documentation aligned to the EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF.

Documentation Audits & Gap Analysis

A structured review of what you already have — flagging outdated steps, missing sign-offs, and procedures that exist nowhere but in someone's head.

Rollout & Team Training

Once a procedure is signed off, we help you publish it into your QMS or wiki and brief the team that has to follow it — so it doesn't just sit in a folder.
