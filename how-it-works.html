Process

From messy process to signed-off SOP in four steps

No long onboarding, no software to learn. You give us the raw material. We give you a document your team can follow and your auditors can trust.

Starts with a conversation, not a form

The discovery call is where we learn how your process actually works, before anything gets drafted.

Step 01

Discovery Call

A 30-minute call to understand the process, who's involved, and which regulations or internal policies it needs to satisfy.

Step 02

AI-Assisted Draft

We generate a structured first draft from your notes, recordings, or existing documents, formatted to a consistent template.

Step 03

Expert Review

A reviewer checks the draft against your regulatory requirements and against how the work is actually done, not just how it's supposed to be done.

Step 04

Delivery & Rollout

You receive a version-controlled document ready to publish, plus a short walkthrough for the team that has to use it.
