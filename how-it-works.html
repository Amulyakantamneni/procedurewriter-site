Process

From messy process to signed-off SOP in four steps

No long onboarding, no software to learn. You give us the raw material — we give you a document your team can follow and your auditors can trust.

Step 01

Discovery Call

A 30-minute call to understand the process, who's involved, and which regulations or internal policies it needs to satisfy.

Step 02

AI-Assisted Draft

We generate a structured first draft from your notes, recordings, or existing documents, formatted to a consistent template.

Step 03

Expert Review

A reviewer checks the draft against your regulatory requirements and against how the work is actually done — not just how it's supposed to be done.

Step 04

Delivery & Rollout

You receive a version-controlled document ready to publish, plus a short walkthrough for the team that has to use it.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does a typical engagement take?

It depends on the complexity and number of procedures. A single SOP can often move from discovery call to final draft within a couple of weeks; larger documentation projects are scoped individually on the discovery call.

Do you write from scratch, or update what we already have?

Both. We can start from an existing document and bring it up to a consistent standard, or build a new one from interviews and notes if nothing formal exists yet.

How do you handle confidential or regulated information?

We sign an NDA before any process details are shared, and we scope exactly what source material is needed for the engagement. Ask on the discovery call about handling for your specific compliance requirements.

Who actually reviews the AI-generated draft?

Every draft goes through human review before delivery — checked against the regulatory framework it needs to satisfy and against how the process is actually performed on the ground.

What does this cost?

Pricing depends on scope — number of procedures, complexity, and turnaround time. We'll give you a clear quote after the discovery call, before any work begins.

Ready to start with a discovery call?

It's free, it's 30 minutes, and you'll walk away knowing exactly what a finished SOP would look like for your process.
